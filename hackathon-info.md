A10 HACKATHON: From ATEN-tion to In-TEN-tion: Building Intelligent AI
Systems

**Students must register
via:** [[a10networks.com/hackathon]{.underline}](http://a10networks.com/hackathon)

In-TEN-tion is about purpose-driven AI --- systems that understand
tasks, make informed decisions, and execute reliably in real-world
environments.  

**This hackathon focuses on two core areas: **

1.  **Task-aware routing **--- intelligently directing requests to the
    right models, tools, or policies, while adapting foundation models
    --- particularly smaller, specialized language models (SLMs) ---
    through targeted fine-tuning and post-training alignment techniques
    (such as SFT and related methods) to perform effectively within
    specific domains and real-world constraints.  

2.  **Agentic workflows **--- building autonomous systems that
    orchestrate identity, security, infrastructure, and execution layers
    (IDP, Envoy, MCP, and beyond), using MCP to enable structured
    context exchange, secure coordination, and reliable cross-system
    execution.  

We encourage candidates with strong experience in SLM fine-tuning and
post-alignment techniques for the first track, and those who understand
MCP fundamentals --- how the protocol works, how context flows between
systems, and how to design MCP-native agents and tools --- for the
second track.
